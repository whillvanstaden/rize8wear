FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

1. How do I place an order?

Browse any collection, choose a design, size, colour, ribbon where applicable and quantity, then select Add to cart. Continue shopping to add other designs or sizes to the same cart. Use the shopping-cart icon to edit or remove items, then choose your collection or delivery method at checkout. Free collection is available in Alberton or Midrand; door delivery uses our fixed store fee displayed at checkout. PAXI, Pargo and PUDO can be requested as courier quotes, with the price and real collection point confirmed on WhatsApp before payment. Review the full order and select Order via WhatsApp, then tap Send. We will confirm stock and payment details for the combined order. You can edit the cart before sending; after sending, request any changes in the same WhatsApp chat before paying.

2. How much does delivery cost?

Collection in Alberton or Midrand is FREE. The Courier Guy door delivery has the fixed store fee displayed at checkout, not a live courier quotation. For PAXI, Pargo or PUDO, request a quote through checkout: we will confirm the delivery fee, a real participating collection point and the final total on WhatsApp before payment.

Verwoerdpark, Alberton: Engen garage, 99 Kritzinger Road, Verwoerdpark, Alberton, 1449. Collection hours: 08:00–19:00.

Midrand: Hertford Office Park, Building F, 90 Bekker Street, Midrand. Monday to Friday, 08:00–15:00. Midrand collection is unavailable from 15 December 2026 through 6 January 2027 inclusive and reopens on 7 January 2027.

3. How long will my order take to arrive?

Your order will begin processing within 48 hours of confirmed payment being received.

Please allow up to 3 working days for preparation and dispatch after processing begins, followed by the delivery timeframe confirmed for your selected service.

Delivery times may vary depending on your location.

4. When will my order be processed?

We begin processing orders within 48 hours after payment has cleared and reflects in our account. Proof of payment alone does not confirm payment.

5. How do I choose the correct T-shirt size?

Each collection includes a size chart to help you choose the right fit.

For the best results, measure a T-shirt you already own and compare the chest width and length with our size guide.

Tip: If you're between sizes, choose the larger size for a more comfortable fit.

6. Can I return or exchange a T-shirt if I ordered the wrong size?

We provide size charts to help you make the correct choice.

We don't offer voluntary size exchanges or change-of-mind returns. However, your statutory consumer rights, including applicable online cooling-off rights, remain unaffected.

7. What if my T-shirt arrives damaged or faulty?

Please contact us with your order number, a description of the problem and clear photographs.

Qualifying defective products are covered by South African consumer protection legislation, including the applicable six-month protection period.

8. Can I order matching Christmas T-shirts for my family?

Yes! Our Christmas collection features matching family T-shirts, including personalised family-name designs.

Choose your preferred design and sizes for each family member.

Once your order has been dispatched, we'll provide tracking information where available so you can follow your delivery.
