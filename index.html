T-SHIRT SIZE CHART

Measure a T-shirt you already own for the best fit.

How to measure

  • Chest: Lay the shirt flat and measure straight across from underarm to underarm.
  • Length: Measure from the highest point of the shoulder down to the bottom hem.

Fit tip: If you are between sizes, choose the larger size for a more comfortable fit.
