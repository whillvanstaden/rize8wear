RIZE 8 WEAR – SHIPPING & DELIVERY POLICY

Last updated: 17 November 2026

At RIZE 8 Wear, we aim to get your order to you as quickly and efficiently as possible.

1. Shipping Costs

Choose your delivery method at checkout. Collection in Alberton or Midrand is FREE; collection addresses and hours are listed below. The Courier Guy door-to-door delivery has a flat fee of R99 per order.

Door-to-door delivery costs R99 per order, added once at checkout. Review your selected delivery or collection details and the final total before paying.

Verwoerdpark, Alberton: Engen garage, 99 Kritzinger Road, Verwoerdpark, Alberton, 1449. Collection hours: 08:00–19:00.

Midrand: Hertford Office Park, Building F, 90 Bekker Street, Midrand. Monday to Friday, 08:00–15:00. Midrand collection is unavailable from 12:00 (noon) on 15 December 2026 through 6 January 2027 inclusive and reopens on 7 January 2027.

2. Payment Confirmation

All orders are processed once payment has cleared and reflects in our bank account.

Please note that proof of payment alone does not confirm payment.

3. Order Processing

Your order will begin processing within 48 hours of confirmed payment being received.

Once processing begins, please allow up to 3 working days for your order to be prepared, packed and dispatched.

Working days exclude weekends and South African public holidays.

4. Delivery Timeframes

Delivery and collection arrangements, service availability and estimated timeframes are confirmed for your selected method before payment.

Delivery times are estimates and may be affected by courier delays, public holidays, remote locations or peak shopping periods.

5. Order Tracking

Once your order has been shipped, we will provide tracking information where available so you can follow your delivery.

6. Delivery Information

Please ensure your delivery address, contact number and postal code are correct when placing your order.

Additional courier charges resulting from incorrect delivery information may be payable by the customer where legally permitted.

7. Delayed or Damaged Orders

If your parcel is delayed, lost or arrives damaged, please contact us so we can investigate and resolve the issue.

Your statutory consumer rights remain unaffected.
