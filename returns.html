RIZE 8 WEAR – RETURNS & REFUNDS POLICY

At RIZE 8 Wear, every order matters. We want you to be happy with your purchase, so please read our returns policy before placing your order.

1. Choosing the Correct Size

We provide a size chart to help you select the correct fit before ordering.

Please check your measurements carefully, as we do not offer voluntary returns or exchanges for:

  • Ordering the incorrect size.
  • Changing your mind after purchasing.
  • Personal preferences regarding fit, colour or style.

This does not affect your rights under South African consumer protection legislation.

2. Defective or Faulty Products

If your T-shirt has a manufacturing defect, such as faulty stitching, split seams, fabric holes or improper colour running, please contact us.

Under the Consumer Protection Act, qualifying defective goods may be returned within six months of delivery for a repair, replacement or refund, at the customer's choice, subject to the applicable legal requirements.

3. Incorrect Items Delivered

If we send you the wrong size, design, colour or product compared with your confirmed order, we will correct the mistake at no additional cost to you.

Please contact us as soon as possible, preferably within seven days of receiving your order, with your order number and photographs of the item.

This notification period does not limit your statutory rights.

4. Online Orders and Cooling-Off Rights

Under South Africa's Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, qualifying online purchases may be cancelled within seven days of receiving the goods, without providing a reason.

However, certain goods, including qualifying personalised or custom-made products, may be excluded from this cooling-off right.

Where the statutory cooling-off right applies, refunds and return arrangements will be handled in accordance with the Act.

5. Custom and Personalised Orders

T-shirts produced with custom names, personalised designs or customer-specific requirements cannot ordinarily be returned because of a change of mind or an incorrect size selection.

Faulty, defective or incorrectly supplied personalised products remain covered by applicable consumer protection laws.

6. How to Report a Problem

If you experience a genuine issue with your order, please contact us with:

  • Your order number.
  • A description of the problem.
  • Clear photographs showing the issue.

Email: info@rize8wear.co.za

We will review the information and assist you with the appropriate next steps.

7. Our Commitment

We stand behind the quality of our products and will resolve genuine manufacturing defects and order fulfilment errors fairly.

Please check our size chart before ordering.
