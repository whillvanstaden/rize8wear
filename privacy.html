Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

RIZE8 Wear respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we use when you visit our website, contact us or order clothing, why we use it, and the choices available to you. It is intended to be read alongside South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who to contact

RIZE8 Wear is responsible for the customer information collected through this shop. Contact us with privacy questions, requests or concerns:

2. Information we collect

  • Customer and delivery details: your full name, email address, contact number, street address, optional unit or complex details, suburb, town or city, province and postal code.
  • Order details: the products, sizes, colours, ribbon selections, quantities, prices, delivery charge and order reference.
  • Messages: information you choose to send us by email, WhatsApp or another contact channel.
  • Payment records, when online payments are enabled: the payment reference, amount and payment status supplied by PayFast. Our checkout does not collect or store your complete card number, card security code or banking password.
  • Technical information: our hosting and security providers may process your IP address, browser information and request logs to deliver and protect the website. The payment service uses a hashed IP address to limit repeated checkout attempts.

Fields marked with an asterisk in checkout are required to process and deliver an order. You can browse without providing these details, but we cannot arrange delivery without complete and accurate customer information.

3. Why we use your information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, prepare and manage orders, arrange delivery, communicate about stock and payment, confirm successful payments, resolve order problems, maintain necessary business records and protect the shop against misuse. Depending on the purpose, processing is necessary for your requested order, a legal obligation, a legitimate business interest or your consent where required.

We do not sell your personal information. Placing an order does not automatically sign you up for promotional messages. We will only send direct marketing where legally permitted and will respect requests to stop it. Supporting our awareness initiatives does not require us to share your customer details with a charity or campaign organiser.

4. Who may receive information

  • Delivery providers: the details needed to deliver your parcel and contact you about delivery.
  • PayFast, when enabled: customer contact details, the order reference, description, delivery details and total needed to process and identify the payment. Payment information entered on PayFast is handled by PayFast under its own privacy policy.
  • Service providers: website hosting, payment-service hosting, database, email and communications providers, including GitHub, Cloudflare and the services supporting our hosted checkout.
  • Authorities or professional advisers: where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights.

Our services may use infrastructure outside South Africa. Where information is transferred internationally, appropriate protection is required under applicable law. External websites and services, including WhatsApp, email providers and PayFast, have their own privacy practices.

5. Email orders and online payments

While email ordering is in use, the final order button opens your email application with the order and customer details filled in. You must send that email for us to receive it; opening the email application does not confirm an order or a payment.

When PayFast checkout is active, the payment service stores your order and delivery details with an awaiting-payment status before redirecting you to PayFast. An order is marked paid only after the service verifies PayFast’s payment notification. Merely returning to the website does not confirm payment.

6. Browser storage, cookies and third-party content

When you continue to online payment, checkout may save a temporary order draft in your browser’s session storage so you can return and edit it after cancelling payment. This draft can contain your contact and address details. Session storage normally lasts for the current browser tab session and can be cleared through your browser settings.

We have not added advertising trackers or analytics scripts to this storefront. Hosting, security, communications and payment services may use their own essential cookies or similar technologies. The main shop loads fonts from Google Fonts, which involves a connection to Google’s servers and may disclose technical information such as your IP address.

7. Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, including HTTPS and restricted access to payment credentials and order records. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Please do not send card security codes or banking passwords to us by email or WhatsApp.

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for orders, customer support, payment reconciliation, disputes and applicable legal recordkeeping duties. Information should then be deleted or de-identified when there is no lawful reason to retain it. Some records may remain in service-provider backups for their normal backup cycle.

8. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal information, ask for corrections or deletion, object to processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Withdrawal does not undo earlier lawful processing. We may need to verify your identity, and some records may need to be retained by law.

Send requests to sales@rize8wear.co.za. You may also lodge a complaint with South Africa’s Information Regulator or email POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za.

9. Children’s information

Our children’s clothing should be ordered by an adult. We do not need a child’s name, birth date or contact details to supply a selected clothing size. Please use the ordering adult’s contact and delivery details.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the shop and its services change. The latest version and its update date will be available on this page. Material changes affecting how we use your information will be communicated where required.
