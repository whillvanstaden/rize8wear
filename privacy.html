RIZE 8 WEAR – PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 17 November 2026

At RIZE 8 Wear, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your information when you visit our website or purchase our products.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit our website, place an order or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your name and surname.
  • Email address and contact number.
  • Delivery and billing addresses.
  • Order details and purchase history.
  • Payment confirmation and transaction information.
  • Website usage information, including cookies and device information.

We only collect information reasonably necessary to operate our business and provide our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Process and fulfil orders.
  • Arrange delivery of purchased products.
  • Communicate about orders, payments and deliveries.
  • Respond to customer enquiries.
  • Handle returns, refunds and customer support.
  • Improve our website and customer experience.
  • Comply with applicable legal and financial obligations.
  • Send promotional communications where permitted by law.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Payment Security

Payments may be processed through secure third-party payment providers.

RIZE 8 Wear does not intentionally collect or store full bank card details on its website.

Payment providers process payment information according to their own security and privacy policies.

4. Sharing Your Information

We may share necessary personal information with trusted service providers, including:

  • Courier and delivery companies.
  • Payment processing providers.
  • Website hosting and technical service providers.
  • Other service providers involved in fulfilling your order.

We only share information necessary for these services and require appropriate protection of personal information.

We may also disclose information when legally required.

5. Cookies and Website Tracking

We save your cart selections and order reference in your browser for up to seven days so you can continue shopping. This may include a surname you choose to print on a T-shirt. The saved cart does not contain your checkout contact details or delivery address. Customer and delivery details entered at checkout remain available while the page is open and are included in the order message when you continue to WhatsApp.

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, understand website traffic and measure marketing performance.

These technologies may include website analytics and advertising tools, where installed.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Certain website features may not function correctly if cookies are disabled.

We use the Meta Pixel (ID 1928208843860853) to record page visits and support advertising measurement. The pixel loads Meta’s tracking script and sends PageView events to Meta. Meta may use cookies and device information in connection with these events. Learn more in Meta’s Privacy Policy.

6. Marketing Communications

We may send you promotional information about new collections, special offers and product updates where you have consented or where otherwise permitted by law.

You may opt out of direct marketing communications at any time by contacting us or using an unsubscribe option where provided.

7. Protecting Your Information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

8. How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil its purpose or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Information that is no longer required will be securely deleted, destroyed or de-identified, subject to legal retention requirements.

9. Your Rights Under POPIA

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of inaccurate or unlawfully retained information.
  • Object to certain processing of your personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Requests may be submitted using our contact details below.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to collect personal information directly from children without appropriate consent or another lawful basis.

Where required, we obtain consent from a competent person before processing a child's personal information.

11. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites, including Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external platforms. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our business practices, website functionality or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on our website.

13. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, requests or concerns, please contact:

RIZE 8 Wear

Website: www.rize8wear.co.za

Email: info@rize8wear.co.za

For unresolved concerns, you may contact the Information Regulator of South Africa at www.inforegulator.org.za.
